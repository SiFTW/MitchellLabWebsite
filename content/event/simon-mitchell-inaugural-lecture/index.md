Simon Mitchell, Professor in Cancer Systems Biology at Brighton and Sussex Medical School (BSMS), presented his inaugural lecture, ***Predicting cancer, personalising care. Can computer simulations find the right treatment?*** on Thursday 19 November 2026 in the Chowen Lecture Theatre at the BSMS Teaching Building.

More than 150 people were in attendance to hear how computer simulations and virtual tumour models can help predict cancer progression and identify the most effective treatment for individual patients, and what this could mean for the future of personalised medicine.

### About the Lecture

No two people are the same, and neither are any two cancers.

In this lecture, Simon Mitchell reveals how virtual tumours can help find the right treatment for each cancer — showing how computer simulation can predict how cancer might progress, or which treatment might work best for each person. Using the same combination of maths and computing used to predict the weather and the stock market, he explores how computer simulation is paving the way for a new era of personalised medicine.
