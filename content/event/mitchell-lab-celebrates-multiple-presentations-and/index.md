A fantastic showing for the Mitchell Lab at the 7th annual NFκB Congress in Barcelona!

PhD students Abigail Edwards and Isabelle Jupp presented their PhD research as posters, which were both very well received. Postdoctoral researcher Dr Aimilia Vareli delivered a short talk presenting the latest findings from her postdoc and was awarded **Best Presentation** at the Congress. In addition, Prof Simon Mitchell gave an invited talk on precision targeting cancer using systems biology approaches.
