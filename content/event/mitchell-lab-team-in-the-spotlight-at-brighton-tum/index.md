The Mitchell Lab had a fantastic showing at the **Brighton Tumour Microenvironment (TME) 2026** conference, with team members highlighted across multiple sessions:

* **Isabelle Jupp** took the spotlight with a selected talk on using systems biology approaches to unravel triple-negative breast cancer, delivering a standout presentation.
* **Oleksandra Goma** presented the fantastic research she accomplished with the lab over the summer.
* **Simon Mitchell** highlighted the latest work from **Abigail Edwards** and **Dr Aimilia Vareli** focused on dissecting B-cell lymphoma dynamics.

We are incredibly proud of the team for showcasing how our computational and systems biology workflows tackle aggressive malignancies!
