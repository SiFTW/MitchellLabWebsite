abstract: "<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>\n                  <jats:p>Balancing\
  \ cell death is essential to maintain healthy tissue homeostasis and prevent disease.\
  \ Tumor necrosis factor (TNF) not only activates nuclear factor \u03BAB (NF\u03BA\
  B), which coordinates the cellular response to inflammation, but may also trigger\
  \ necroptosis, a pro\u2010inflammatory form of cell death. Whether TNF\u2010induced\
  \ NF\u03BAB affects the fate decision to undergo TNF\u2010induced necroptosis is\
  \ unclear. Live\u2010cell microscopy and model\u2010aided analysis of death kinetics\
  \ identified a molecular circuit that interprets TNF\u2010induced NF\u03BAB/RelA\
  \ dynamics to control necroptosis decisions. Inducible expression of TNFAIP3/A20\
  \ forms an incoherent feedforward loop to interfere with the RIPK3\u2010containing\
  \ necrosome complex and protect a fraction of cells from transient, but not long\u2010\
  term TNF exposure. Furthermore, dysregulated NF\u03BAB dynamics often associated\
  \ with disease diminish TNF\u2010induced necroptosis. Our results suggest that TNF's\
  \ dual roles in either coordinating cellular responses to inflammation, or further\
  \ amplifying inflammation are determined by a dynamic NF\u03BAB\u2010A20\u2010RIPK3\
  \ circuit, that could be targeted to treat inflammation and cancer.</jats:p>"
