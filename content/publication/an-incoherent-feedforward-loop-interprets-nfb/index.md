abstract: "<jats:title>ABSTRACT</jats:title>\n                <jats:p>Balancing cell\
  \ death is essential to maintain healthy tissue homeostasis and prevent disease.\
  \ Tumor necrosis factor (TNF) not only activates nuclear factor \u03BAB (NF\u03BA\
  B), which coordinates the cellular response to inflammation, but may also trigger\
  \ necroptosis, a pro-inflammatory form of cell death. Whether TNF-induced NF\u03BA\
  B cross-regulates TNF-induced necroptosis fate decisions is unclear. Live-cell microscopy\
  \ and model-aided analysis of death kinetics identified a molecular circuit that\
  \ interprets TNF-induced NF\u03BAB/RelA dynamics to control necroptosis decisions.\
  \ Inducible expression of TNFAIP3/A20 forms an incoherent feedforward loop to interfere\
  \ with the RIPK3-containing necrosome complex and protect a fraction of cells from\
  \ transient, but not long-term TNF exposure. Furthermore, dysregulated NF\u03BA\
  B dynamics often associated with disease diminish TNF-induced necroptosis. Our results\
  \ suggest that TNF\u2019s dual roles in either coordinating cellular responses to\
  \ inflammation, or further amplifying inflammation are determined by a dynamic NF\u03BA\
  B-A20-RIPK3 circuit, that could be targeted to treat inflammation and cancer.</jats:p>"
