abstract: "**Significance**\n\n \n *Staphylococcus aureus*\n (SA) is the leading cause\
  \ of skin and skin structure infections (SSSI), which are the main portal of entry\
  \ for life-threatening invasive infections. Treatment failures are increasingly\
  \ common due to antibiotic resistance. Importantly, SA SSSI exhibit high 1-year\
  \ recurrence, despite high antibody levels against staphylococcal antigens. Therefore,\
  \ critical determinants of immune protection against recurrent SA SSSI are not well\
  \ understood. The present study offers important insights in this area, including:\
  \ (\n *i*\n ) macrophages can confer protective memory transferrable to naïve hosts;\
  \ (\n *ii*\n ) SA SSSI can induce memory in bone marrow myeloid precursors; and\
  \ (\n *iii*\n ) specific memory against recurring SA SSSI is localized and distinct\
  \ from disseminated infection. These findings will accelerate novel strategies to\
  \ prevent and treat SA infections."
